SimplySolitaire Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

SimplySolitaire is a classic solitaire card game for Android. It is designed to respect your privacy completely.

Information we collect

None. SimplySolitaire does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information or usage data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Internet access

The app does not request permission to use the internet, so it cannot send any information off your device.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves your game in progress, your chosen settings (theme and number of cards to draw) and your game statistics. This information stays on your device. It is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings.

If you have turned on Android's own backup feature, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your device settings; the developer of SimplySolitaire cannot access it.

Permissions

SimplySolitaire does not request any device permissions.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
