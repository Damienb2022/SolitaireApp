100% offline, no ads. Just SimplySolitaire.

The classic Klondike solitaire you remember: a green table, a deck of cards, and nothing else.

No ads. No pop-ups. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No tracking. The app doesn't even ask for internet access, so it works anywhere (on a plane, in the bush, with Wi-Fi off) and it can't send anything anywhere.

EASY TO PLAY
• Tap a card and it jumps to the best place, or drag it where you want
• Big, clear cards and large buttons
• Unlimited Undo
• Hint button shows you a good move
• Tells you when a game can't be won, so you're never stuck guessing
• Finishes the game for you once every card is turned over

CLASSIC FEEL
• Draw 1 (easier) or Draw 3 (classic challenge)
• The bouncing-cards celebration when you win
• Choose a table colour: Classic green, Blue, Orange or Dark

KEEP TRACK
• Games played, won, lost and discarded
• Win rate, current and best winning streak
• Fewest moves to win

Your game is saved after every move, so you can pick up exactly where you left off.
